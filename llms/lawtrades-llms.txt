# LawTrades

> LawTrades is a legal talent marketplace, founded in 2016, that matches companies with
> vetted independent attorneys, paralegals, and legal operations professionals on flexible,
> on-demand terms. The platform handles matching, work-log management, invoicing, payments,
> and real-time spend analytics, and layers a human-in-the-loop AI product ("AI drafts,
> lawyers decide") over contract and compliance work. LawTrades publishes no public API,
> developer portal, SDKs, or machine-readable API descriptions as of this writing.

## Status

- No public API: LawTrades exposes no documented REST/GraphQL API, no developer portal, and no
  API reference. `api.lawtrades.com`, `docs.lawtrades.com`, and `developer(s).lawtrades.com`
  do not resolve (NXDOMAIN, probed 2026-07-19).
- No `/llms.txt` is published by the provider; this file is generated by the API Evangelist
  enrichment pipeline from the catalog entry and repo artifacts.
